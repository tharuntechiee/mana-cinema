Privacy notice

Version of 27 October 2026

You can read everything on MANA CINEMA without an account. You need one only to rate films, comment and like comments, so that each person has one rating per film and the scores stay fair. This notice says what we keep when you sign in, why, and how to get it back or delete it.

What we keep

  • Signing in with Google: Google's ID for your account, your first name (which you can change) and your email address. We never see your Google password.
  • Signing in with an email code: your email address. The code is sent from MANA CINEMA's own email account; we keep only a scrambled form of it, and only until you use it or it expires 10 minutes later.
  • What you post: your ratings, reviews, comments and likes, with the name you chose.
  • Sign-ins: when you signed in and were last here, so we can keep you signed in for 30 days and let you sign out everywhere. We keep a scrambled form of the sign-in cookie, not the cookie itself.

What we use it for

Only to run your account, send your sign-in codes, show your name on what you post, and allow one rating per person for each film. Everything is kept in MANA CINEMA's own database. We don't sell or share your details, show ads based on them, or send you marketing emails.

Your choices

  • Download your data: account menu › Download my data.
  • Change your name: account menu.
  • Delete your account: account menu › Delete my account. This deletes your account, ratings, comments (with their replies) and likes straight away, and signs you out everywhere. Deleting is how you withdraw your consent.

Who can see what

Everyone sees your chosen name with your ratings and comments. Only you and the site's admin see your email address. The admin can hide posts or block an account that breaks the rules.

Age

Accounts are for people aged 18 or over.

Questions and complaints

Write to us through the feedback form (choose "Other feedback"). We'll answer within 90 days, and usually much sooner. If something goes wrong with your data, we'll tell you without delay. You can also complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
