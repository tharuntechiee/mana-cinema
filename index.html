    How we track the numbers

    We cover Indian cinema in nine languages: Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Malayalam, Kannada, Marathi, Bengali, Punjabi and Gujarati. Day-wise India net comes from trade trackers, mostly Sacnilk, cross-checked with Koimoi and Bollywood Hungama; the all-time lists start from Wikipedia's language lists and are checked against the same trackers.

    For older and smaller films we list the reported totals and opening day; day-by-day figures are shown where trackers publish them.

    Every figure is a trade estimate, not an audited number. Makers' claims are shown separately on film pages and are never ranked. A day nobody reported is left blank, never counted as zero, so running totals stop at the gap.

    Verdicts come from the trade. Until a film has one, we show an indicative verdict based on its lifetime India net against its budget (Koimoi's bands). An asterisk (*) marks an indicative verdict, or a film still in theatres whose verdict may change.

    India net
    Box-office takings in India after GST. Every day-wise figure here is India net.
    India gross
    India takings including GST, usually 12 to 18% above net.
    Worldwide gross
    India gross plus overseas gross. Net and gross are never mixed.
    Paid previews (Day 0)
    Premieres the night before release, reported apart from Day 1. Counted in the opening unless you switch them off.
    Opening weekend
    Release day to the first Sunday, so a Thursday release gets a 4-day weekend. Monday and Tuesday releases aren't ranked on it; compare them on First 7 days.
    Trade week
    Friday to Thursday. A Thursday release has an 8-day first week.
    Verdict
    The trade's call on how a film did against its cost, from Disaster to All-Time Blockbuster.
    Ratings
    Critics' average out of 10, an IMDb snapshot, and audience stars saved in this browser.
